## Project name:## E-Commerce Logistics Data Analytics and Delivery Optimization
## Objective:## Analyze e-commerce orders, delivery performance, freight costs, and customer/seller locations; identify operational issues; and build a model to predict delivery performance and support logistics optimization.

- Week 1: Project strategy, business questions, and KPIs.
- Week 2: Data collection, cleaning, and preprocessing.
- Week 3: Exploratory data analysis and visualizations.
- Week 4: Predictive modeling, evaluation, and optimization recommendations.
